Defined in code, not sent

  • BOOKING_CONFIRMED — unpaid create sends only the pay-link SMS. Confirmation text goes out later as BOOKING_PAID. The unused body can also include a Google Maps URL and the receipt URL.
  • OWNER_BOOKING_PAID — payment uses OWNER_BOOKING_CONFIRMED instead.
  • PASSWORD_RESET — forgot-password sends the OTP SMS, not a reset link.
